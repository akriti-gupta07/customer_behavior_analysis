# Customer Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes customer shopping behavior to uncover purchasing patterns, customer preferences, product performance, and sales trends. The project uses **Python** for data cleaning and preprocessing, **MySQL** for business analysis, and **Power BI** for creating an interactive dashboard.

## Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior helps businesses improve marketing strategies, customer engagement, product offerings, and overall sales performance. This project analyzes customer shopping data to identify meaningful trends related to customer demographics, purchasing habits, product categories, discounts, subscriptions, payment methods, and customer loyalty.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting with raw data exploration and cleaning, followed by SQL-based analysis and interactive data visualization.

## Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing patterns and preferences
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Understand customer behavior across different demographic groups
* Compare subscribed and non-subscribed customers
* Analyze the impact of discounts and promotional offers
* Evaluate customer loyalty and purchase frequency
* Identify actionable insights for data-driven business decisions

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection

The customer shopping behavior dataset was obtained from the dataset provided with the referenced data analytics tutorial.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

Python and Pandas were used to prepare the dataset for analysis. The preprocessing steps included:

* Understanding the structure of the dataset
* Checking data types
* Identifying missing values
* Handling missing data
* Standardizing column names
* Removing unnecessary columns
* Creating derived columns
* Preparing the cleaned dataset for SQL analysis

### 3. SQL Analysis

The cleaned dataset was imported into a MySQL database. SQL queries were used to answer business-related questions and identify important customer trends.

The analysis focused on:

* Customer purchasing behavior
* Revenue contribution by customer segments
* Product and category performance
* Subscription behavior
* Discount usage
* Customer loyalty
* Payment preferences
* Shipping preferences
* Purchase frequency

### 4. Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to present the results visually. The dashboard provides an overview of customer behavior through key performance indicators, charts, filters, and visualizations.

The dashboard enables users to explore:

* Total customers
* Average purchase amount
* Average customer rating
* Revenue by category
* Customer distribution by subscription status
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Product and category performance

## Key Business Questions

The project addresses questions such as:

1. What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
2. Which customers used discounts but spent more than the average purchase amount?
3. Which products received the highest average customer ratings?
4. How do purchase amounts differ between standard and express shipping?
5. Do subscribed customers spend more than non-subscribed customers?
6. Which products have the highest percentage of purchases made using discounts?
7. How can customers be categorized based on their purchase frequency?
8. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
9. How many repeat customers have made more than five previous purchases?
10. What is the revenue contribution of different customer age groups?

## Key Insights

* Customer demographics influence purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
* Product categories contribute differently to overall revenue.
* Subscription status can affect customer engagement and purchasing behavior.
* Discounts and promotional offers influence purchasing decisions.
* Customer purchase frequency helps identify loyal and repeat customers.
* Product ratings provide insights into customer satisfaction and product performance.
* Payment and shipping preferences help businesses understand customer convenience and service expectations.
